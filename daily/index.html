How to play

Tap a square and its tank fills with water up to there. Tap the top of the water to lower it.

Water lies flat: each tank has one water line, and every square of the tank on or below it is water.

The numbers say how many water squares each row and column holds. A ? can hold any amount.

Too much water? Tap the top of it to lower it a row, or Undo. Every board has one answer, and logic always finds it.

Best is the fewest taps possible: one per tank that holds water. Match it for a Perfect.
