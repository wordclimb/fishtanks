Fish Tanks: Privacy Policy

Star Ladder Games · Effective date: October 25, 2026

Fish Tanks is a logic puzzle game for Android, with a free Daily Tank you can also play in a web browser, made by Star Ladder Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: your progress stays on your phone (or in your browser); the free app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google handles the ads and any purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, hints, fish), your Daily Tank results and streak, and your settings are saved only on your phone, in the game's private storage. We don't receive them and there's no account. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

The Fish Tanks app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and a video ad when you choose to watch one for a free hint. There are no banner ads. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.

Buying Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack) turns off the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a free hint stay available, only if you want them.

Purchases

In-app purchases (the Starter Pack, Remove Ads and pearl packs) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game which items your account owns so they can be delivered and restored on a new phone.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your phone for when a new Daily Tank is ready. It's scheduled and shown by your phone; nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

What we collect

We (Star Ladder Games) don't run servers for the game and don't collect your personal information. The game has no analytics of its own. The ad and purchase information above is collected by Google under its own policies. If a future version adds crash reports or usage statistics, this policy and the game's Data safety section on Google Play will be updated first.

The web Daily Tank

The web version of the Daily Tank keeps your progress and streak in your browser's local storage on your device; we don't receive it. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts, so those services see your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. The web page shows no ads.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share after a Daily Tank, the game hands the text you see (your taps as small fish and bubbles, par, time and streak) to your phone's share sheet or your clipboard. It goes only to the app and people you choose. Fish Tanks doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Fish Tanks isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with the game's Data safety section on Google Play updated to match, before the version that needs it is released.
